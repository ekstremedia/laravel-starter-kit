# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We expect everyone interacting in the project's spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channels — to act respectfully and in good faith.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming to newcomers.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and giving/accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without consent, and anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Adoption

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1**. Please read it there for the full standards and enforcement guidelines — we follow them rather than reproducing the text here.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer [@ekstremedia](https://github.com/ekstremedia). Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
